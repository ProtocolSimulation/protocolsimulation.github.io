PLAYER SUPPORT / PRIVACY

Request access or deletion.

Contact the studio regarding your personal data or an account associated with a game.

How to submit a request

Send an email to policepatrol.666@gmail.com with the game name, the type of request (access, correction, deletion or account deletion), and enough information to identify the relevant account if applicable. Do not include passwords, payment details or unrelated sensitive information.

What happens next?

We may need further information to verify the request. The applicable options depend on the game and its data practices. Check the game's Google Play data safety information. This page provides a contact route; it does not automatically delete an account or its data. Uninstalling an app may remove local data but does not necessarily remove information held by third parties or online services.
