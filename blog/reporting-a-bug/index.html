How to send useful game feedback

Help us understand a problem with a clear and reproducible report.

← All articles

Include the exact game title, your device model, Android version and app version if you know it. Describe what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.

If possible, include the sequence of actions that reproduces the problem and an image or recording that does not expose private information. Use our support page to prepare an email.
