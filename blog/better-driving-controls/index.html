Getting comfortable with driving controls

Practical tips for learning the controls in a mobile simulator.

← All articles

Spend a few minutes learning the steering, throttle and brake controls before attempting fast corners. Begin on a wide road or open area and try smooth inputs.

If a game offers separate driving modes, switch between them and notice how the handling changes. Adjust settings gradually when available. Take a screenshot of a setup you like so you can reproduce it later.
