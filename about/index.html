ENGINEERING / TOOLS & SERVICES

Technology stack

From vehicle simulation and real-time graphics to multiplayer, mobile publishing, monetization and performance analysis: the technologies behind Protocol Simulation.

Game engineering Graphics & rendering 3D art & asset production Multiplayer & online services Mobile platform & publishing Advertising & monetization Analytics & performance Web engineering
01

Game engineering

The core engine, programming language and vehicle simulation systems.

Game engineUnity
Core environment for building game scenes, gameplay systems, physics integration and Android builds.
Gameplay programmingC#
Implementation of gameplay logic, vehicle controls, user interfaces and game-specific systems within Unity.
Vehicle physicsRealistic Car Controller V3 (RCC V3)
Vehicle simulation foundation extended with in-house modifications to adapt driving behaviour and controls to individual games.
02

Graphics & rendering

Rendering pipelines selected according to project and platform requirements.

Mobile renderingUniversal Render Pipeline (URP)
Increasingly used for real-time graphics, lighting, materials and visual effects on Android.
Existing projectsBuilt-in Render Pipeline
Retained in selected games using the original Unity rendering architecture.
03

3D art & asset production

Tools and resources for creating and integrating game-ready content.

3D asset productionBlender
Creation, editing and preparation of 3D models and other assets for integration into Unity.
Asset sourcingUnity Asset Store
Integration of third-party models, environments, tools and supporting assets into game projects.
04

Multiplayer & online services

Cloud services supporting connected gameplay and competition.

Multiplayer networkingPhoton Cloud
Cloud-hosted multiplayer connectivity and real-time online game sessions.
Online rankingsUnity Gaming Services — Leaderboards
Hosted storage and ranking of player scores for leaderboard functionality.
05

Mobile platform & publishing

Target platform and distribution infrastructure.

Target platformAndroid
Mobile game development and deployment for Android devices.
DistributionGoogle Play
Public game distribution, store listings and delivery of game releases and updates.
06

Advertising & monetization

Advertising mediation and demand networks supporting free-to-play games.

Ad mediationUnity LevelPlay (ironSource)
Management and mediation of participating mobile advertising networks.
Ad networkGoogle AdMob
Mobile advertising demand and monetization.
Ad networkMeta Audience Network
Advertising demand through Meta’s mobile app monetization network.
Ad networkUnity Ads
In-game advertising and monetization through Unity’s advertising network.
07

Analytics & performance

Monitoring game performance, player activity and advertising results.

Store & app analyticsGoogle Play Console
Review of installs, acquisition, app statistics and Android vitals, including crashes and ANRs.
Gameplay analyticsUnity Analytics
Analysis of game events and player engagement to inform development decisions.
Monetization analyticsUnity LevelPlay
Monitoring advertising revenue, impressions and mediation performance.
Runtime performance profilingUnity Profiler
Analysis of CPU, memory, rendering and other runtime performance metrics to identify bottlenecks.
08

Web engineering

The technologies powering the official studio website.

Content & structureHTML5
Semantic website pages, game information, galleries and support resources.
Visual designCSS3
Responsive layouts, typography, visual styling and interactions across screen sizes.
Client-side functionalityJavaScript
Catalogue search, filters, sorting, navigation, galleries and support form behaviour.
Static hostingGitHub Pages
Hosting and distribution of the official website through a static deployment workflow.

Technology usage varies across titles and project requirements.
